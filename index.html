- How Moncem works

Three motions. One feed worth returning to.

01

Post the real build.

Share a journey, build, idea, or demo with the context founders actually care about - stage, proof, tags, and ask.

02

Signal what resonates.

Spark, validate, or say "in this" so good stories turn into momentum instead of disappearing into a feed.

03

Start the right conversation.

Connect while the story is fresh. Ask about the numbers, the decision, the thing they learned the hard way.

- Built for founders

Not another social feed. A field log for people in motion.

- FOUNDER MEMO

Stories with numbers in the foreground.

Milestones, stages, and proof sit beside the narrative so readers understand why the story matters fast.

- MARKET MAP

Explore what's moving this week.

Browse active topics, discover builders open to connect, and see where momentum is forming - by tag, by stage, by region.

- REACTIONS

Spark, validate, or join the build.

Three reaction types designed for founder feedback - not vanity engagement. The "in this" is the most powerful.

- CONVERSATION CONTEXT

Inbox threads start from a story.

Messages keep the post context attached, so every conversation begins with shared signal - never cold opens.

- The promise

Open the app and know what builders are actually doing.

Moncem is for the moments founders usually bury in scattered notes, group chats, and screenshots - first customer, failed pricing test, shipped prototype, investor no, user interview, unexpected traction.

  • For early founders - get inspired by specific, honest build stories.
  • For operators - find builders working on problems you understand.
  • For curious people - see how products are actually born.
